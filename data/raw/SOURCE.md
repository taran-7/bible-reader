|---|---|---|---|---|

Format of the files: an array of 66 books `{abbrev, name, chapters: [[verse, …], …]}` in Protestant order (index + 1 = book number).

`ru_synodal.json` is generated by `scripts/convert_synodal.py`: of the source's 78 books, the 66 canonical ones are taken, empty verses (Ps 114:9) are dropped. Chapter counts are kept as in the Synodal (Dan 14, Ps 151).

Why not `thiagobodruk/bible` `ru_synod.json`: it lacks Esther and Daniel (64 books).

`uk_ohienko.json` and `cs_bkr.json` are generated by `scripts/convert_getbible.py` (`python3 scripts/convert_getbible.py UBIO.json data/raw/uk_ohienko.json --align-to data/raw/en_kjv.json`; converter tests are `scripts/test_convert_getbible.py`, run by `make test`): books by the `nr` field, without U+0301 stress marks and `<…>` tags, in BKR alternative words `{…}` → `(…)`, missing verses are empty strings (the import skips them, numbers are kept).

Known specifics:
- **Ohienko:** numbering aligned with KJV verse by verse (`--align-to data/raw/en_kjv.json`), 31,102 verses, as many as KJV in every chapter. The source uses Hebrew numbering, so the converter merges a psalm superscription (1–2 verses, in 62 psalms) into the first verse of the text, as in KJV; joins 1 Sam 21:1 to 20:42; merges 3 John 1:14–15 into one verse 14. Any other mismatch stops the conversion. Quotes „…“ (ASCII `"` in the source converted), italics `<i>…</i>` removed, square brackets […] kept as in the source.
- **Why not getBible `ukrogienko`:** it is missing 88 verses (mostly psalm superscriptions), books are unordered, the text has stress marks.
- **Rights to Ohienko:** getBible marks the Ohienko text as Public Domain, bolls.life states no license, but Ohienko died in 1972, and in Ukraine protection lasts 70 years after the author's death. Before distribution, verify the rights holder of the 1962 edition (PRD §9, tech debt).
- **BKR:** KJV numbering, 31,102 verses.
